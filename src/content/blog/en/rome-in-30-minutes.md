Got a day in Rome but a packed schedule? You don't need days for everything. We collected the quests in Roam's Rome set that take 20 to 30 minutes: perfect for a gap between meetings, before a train or ahead of dinner.

## 1. Toss a coin into the Trevi Fountain (30 min)

Fellini filmed *La Dolce Vita* here. The tradition: throw a coin over your shoulder; one for returning to Rome, two for love, three for marriage. To see the fountain without the crowd, go before 7am or after 10pm. At 6am you get the fountain alone, at 11pm the lit-up baroque drama.

## 2. The Aventine Keyhole (20 min)

On quiet Aventine Hill there is a green door belonging to the Knights of Malta. Through its keyhole you see a garden tunnel of cypress trees framing the dome of St. Peter's. Look, gasp, done. Go early in the morning to avoid a queue.

## 3. A gran caffè at Sant'Eustachio (20 min)

A secret kept since 1938: the first drops of espresso are whipped with sugar behind a screen. The result is a small cup of velvet. Drink it standing at the bar; sitting costs triple.

## 4. Gelato at Fatamorgana (30 min)

Rome's most inventive all-natural gelato, with seasonal and strange flavors like Kentucky tobacco, black rice or pear-gorgonzola. Ask to taste three before you decide.

## How to combine them

Walking time between stops counts too, so spreading the four across half a day is more realistic: Trevi very early or late at night, with the coffee in between. For a longer food plan, see [eating in Rome without the tourist traps](/en/blog/rome-food-route/). Opening hours can change.
